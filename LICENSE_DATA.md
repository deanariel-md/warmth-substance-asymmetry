## Source-data provenance

Original forum posts used as scenario sources were de-identified at extraction (usernames, dates, and direct identifiers removed) and minimally adapted to reduce searchability while preserving clinical content and patient voice. Source-forum types and scenario provenance categories are described in the manuscript's supplementary appendix (Table S3). **Specific forum names, domains, direct source URLs, usernames, post dates, and verbatim source-post text are not released.** Materials withheld from this release are not covered by this license; CC-BY 4.0 applies only to the files actually contained in `data/`. The activity used only publicly available information and did not generate or use individually identifiable private information about living individuals; no IRB review was sought (45 CFR 46.102(e)).
